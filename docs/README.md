### Week 1

I ended up changing some things around a little bit. I have decided to do a hero vs villain game based on Marvel heroes and villains. Basically every character is going to have a certain amount of health and damage. To make it more fun, I added weapons and armor that you can choose from. The weapons will have additional damage but also less defense. The armor will have more defense but less damage. The idea is that you pick a loadout and then you fight. You can either attack or defend. Depending on your choice, the computer will also choose one randomly and that will determine what happens. My thinking is that if you defend and the other villain attacks then you will take reduced damage and vice versa. The main obstacles that i found was just thinking about the logic of how the game would work. I overcame this by writing down how attack and defend will work. I also created the classes: Characters, Weapons, and Armor which greatly helped me understand how everything was going to work. I have definitely got a better grasp on how to plan out things. I feel confident in starting projects now because I realized that it takes time to plan out everything, but once it has been planned out, it makes it a lot easier to start programming. I am going to prioritize the logic behind the weapons and armor. (how they will effect every character).
